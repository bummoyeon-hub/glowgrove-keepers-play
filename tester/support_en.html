Payments

Version 1.0 is completely free with no paid products (no in-app purchases). All in-game currency comes from play and rewarded ads. If we introduce paid products later, we will amend the terms and announce it in advance.

Saves

Can I move my progress to a new device?

Version 1.0 stores progress only on the device and has no cloud save, so progress cannot be moved and is lost if you delete the app. Cloud save is being considered for a future update.

Deleting data

We keep no copy of your game data (progress is stored only on the device). To delete it, use More [더보기] → Settings [설정] → Reset data [데이터 초기화], or delete the app. Ad-related data is handled by Google under its own policies. Details: Privacy Policy

Ads

Are there ads?

From day 3 after installation (Korea time) or after you first defeat the 1-10 boss, whichever comes first, (1) a banner ad appears at the bottom of the screen and (2) "watch an ad for a reward" buttons appear. Rewarded ads play only when you tap such a button. There are no full-screen interstitial ads. Ads are non-personalized ads from Google AdMob.

I watched an ad but got no reward

Rewards may not be granted if the ad did not finish or was not available; check the daily limit too. If it keeps happening, contact us with the time.

Playing

Where are the odds?

The odds button next to Light the Lighthouse, the odds table on the summon screen, and the odds page.

Idle rewards are missing or small

Idle rewards accumulate for up to 12 hours after you leave; the amount depends on your best stage. They cannot be claimed if the device clock has gone backwards.

Does 10x speed give 10x rewards?

No. Speed only makes battles faster; rewards bank at up to 2x the x1 rate. All speeds are free.

Are the other teams in the rankings real people?

No. In version 1.0 rankings and arena opponents are AI rivals generated by the Game.

Use of AI tools

AI tools were used as an aid in creating some 2D graphic assets; every game resource and the game balance were reviewed and adjusted by the developer.
