Privacy Policy

The 1.1 free tester build also has the paid store disabled. New accounts receive 30 standard guardian summon tickets and 30 lighthouse embers separately. This build has no online rankings.

Yeon Beommo (individual developer, "we") operates the free game Lantern Keepers: Glowgrove (등불 수호단: 글로우그로브, the "Game"). You start without signing up or logging in, and the Game never asks for your name, phone number, email, address, date of birth, contacts or photos.

Version 1.0 has no game server run by us. Your progress is stored only on your device and we do not receive your progress or records.

2. Ads (Google AdMob — banner and rewarded)

  • The Game has two kinds of ads: (1) a banner ad at the bottom of the screen, and (2) rewarded ads that play only when you tap a "watch an ad for a reward" button (e.g. embers, Moonstones, a shorter lighthouse upgrade, double idle rewards — kinds and daily limits as shown in the Game). There are no full-screen interstitial ads.
  • The banner and the ad reward buttons appear from day 3 after installation (Korea time) or after you first defeat the 1-10 boss, whichever comes first.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob and we request non-personalized ads only. The Game does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, so the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used.
  • To show ads, prevent fraud and measure ad performance, Google may process through its SDK: app- or developer-scoped device identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, ads viewed and ad interactions, app launch and performance data, and crash logs. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Why

  • Saving progress and calculating idle rewards
  • Showing ads (a bottom banner, and rewarded ads when you choose to watch one)

4. Retention and deletion

  • Your save stays on the device until you delete the app or use More [더보기] → Settings [설정] → Reset data [데이터 초기화]. Deleting the app erases progress permanently (there is no cloud save in 1.0).
  • We keep no copy of your game data.
  • Google's retention and deletion of ad-related data follow Google's policies.

5. Sharing

  • We do not sell or share your information, except where the law requires.
  • Rewarded ads: Google LLC (AdMob), processing data directly under its own policy. App distribution: Apple Inc. (App Store).

6. Children

The Game does not collect personal information that needs parental consent, and requests non-personalized ads only. Version 1.0 has no paid products.

7. Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, but you can send questions or requests to the contact below. Ad-related choices can be made in your Google account settings and under Google's policies.
